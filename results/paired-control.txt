Notes
-----
The first two rows are the same number twice, and they have to be: the basket is the
borrower's wallet, not her Aave collateral. No fill in either arm touches her aTokens
or her debt, so Aave's health factor cannot move, and on a path that stops at HF 1.10
neither arm is liquidated. What the instruction changes is what she is HOLDING when
she gets to the bottom, and what she was paid or charged on the way. The last three
rows are her own action, taken identically in both arms: repay the USDC debt with the
USDC her basket holds. Freeboard never repays; it puts the USDC in her hand.

Spread: for the Freeboard arm this is what takers paid her above the Aave oracle,
ten basis points a fill. For the unguarded arm it is what takers took from her below
the oracle: a constant-product basket keeps its price until an arbitrageur moves it,
so on a falling oracle it buys the falling asset above market at every rung.

Hold: each arm's own shipped basket, untouched, at the bottom's prices. Both arms
start at the oracle for their instruction, so neither is charged for its seeding:
at HF 2.00 the Freeboard basket is at the top row and no taker has anything to take,
and the unguarded basket's pairs are at the oracle until the first warp moves it.
